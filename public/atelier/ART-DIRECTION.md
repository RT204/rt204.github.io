# Art direction and generated assets

Direction: **Komorebi** (木漏れ日), sunlight filtering through leaves. Warm cream paper, muted sage storefronts, dusty turquoise, afternoon light, a handmade engineering journal, and a quiet anime companion. Generated using the built-in image generation tool; converted to WebP for delivery while preserving the character's alpha channel.

## `assets/atelier-scene.webp`

Original generated file: `exec-15b4f8ff-6b6f-4f81-9150-1ffcf129a8ae.png`. The supplied image was used as mood reference, not as a page background.

Use case: illustration-story. Asset type: ultra-wide painted background for an engineering portfolio website. Reference image 1 is mood and palette reference only. Create a new lovingly hand-painted anime environment, landscape 3:2 or wider. A quiet Japanese neighborhood coffee shop converted into a tiny maker atelier, seen from the street, early afternoon, softly dappled sunlight filtering through lush hanging ivy, faded sage-green metal awning and window frames, old cream plaster, amber window reflections, terracotta plant pots, a small black cat sleeping on a stool, glimpses of a wooden workbench with a small sumo robot and tools through the right window. Warm ivory, celadon, muted eucalyptus, tiny turquoise accents, filmic atmosphere, delicate painted detail with soft luminous edges, composition rich yet restful. Keep LEFT 45% bright misty cream plaster and sunlit window with low detail, suitable for dark typography over it; concentrate the detailed storefront and plants on RIGHT 55%. No humans and no characters (character will be composited separately). No legible text, no logos, no signage lettering, no watermark, no website mockup. Feeling: a summer afternoon you wish would last forever.

## `assets/miku.webp`

Original generated file: `exec-61130d0a-bec6-4aac-87d0-a4fb38d1c9af.png`. Generated with a transparent background. Character artwork uses two complementary CSS masks for a breathing body and independently swaying hair; it is not a rigged Live2D asset.

Use case: illustration-story. Asset type: transparent character layer for a gently animated anime café portfolio website. Create a beautiful full-body illustration of Hatsune Miku in a relaxed casual maker/café outfit: oversized ivory long-sleeve shirt with sleeves rolled once, sage pleated midi skirt, dark low-top shoes, slim dark headphones resting around her neck, very long iconic turquoise twin tails with tiny dark red hair clips, holding a small sketchbook close to her chest. Young adult anime character, quiet thoughtful expression, eyes open looking slightly to her left, gentle small smile. Three-quarter view with body facing a little left, standing relaxed with one knee soft. Both hands anatomically correct. Painterly delicate anime line work, soft watercolor shading and dappled warm sunlight from upper left. Dusty turquoise, sage green, pale cream; restrained colors fitting a sunlit Japanese neighborhood atelier. Long twin tails sweep gracefully toward her right in a light breeze, silhouette clean and easy to animate as a separate layer. Entire body and all hair visible, generous clear transparent padding, no cropping. Actual transparent background, no shadow floor, no background objects, no text, no logos, no watermark. One character only.
